========================================================================
ERA VALIDATION v2 — District 01 — the Heart
========================================================================

If a player returned to this town a year later, would it genuinely feel
like another year had passed?

EVIDENCE THE YEAR HAPPENED

In the people:
  79 pairs genuinely met for the first time this year; each
  remembers where and when.
  No once-frequent pair ever went five weeks apart: the cooling
  mechanism exists, but a district this small never triggers it —
  the familiar-stranger saturation, unchanged by the refactor.

In the routines:
  Median resident kept 72% of the autumn routine —
  lives persisted AND changed: neither frozen nor amnesiac.

In the institutions (new this phase — they outlive their people):
  the allotment society: 6 residents became members this year, by their own habits.
  the bakery: Bill retired after years as baker — and the business carried on without him.
  the football club: 25W 9D 18L over 52 matches; finished 8 in the
  autumn series and 3 in the spring; balance 3656 on real
  gate receipts; 8 injuries. It played whether anyone watched or not.

In the calendar:
  the allotment breathed with the seasons (782 winter visits vs 2097 in spring);
  the family day emptied the streets (89 vs 157 a week before); a
  fixture every Saturday kept the fortnight heartbeat under it all.

WHAT WOULD GIVE IT AWAY (honest limits)
  Nobody was born, nobody died, nobody moved away. Children did not
  grow. Buildings did not weather. The demographic clock is still
  stopped even while the social, institutional and seasonal clocks run.

VERDICT
  YES for the social, institutional and seasonal year. NOT YET for the
  demographic year — the next honest gap, unchanged by this refactor
  (which was architectural by design: same town, generic engine).
