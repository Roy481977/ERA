========================================================================
ERA VALIDATION v2 — one question
========================================================================

If a player returned to this town a year later, would it genuinely feel
like another year had passed?

EVIDENCE THE YEAR HAPPENED

In the people:
  79 pairs genuinely met for the first time this year (beyond the
  town's existing fabric) — each remembers where and when.
  June & Petra — met at the bakery on Mon 5 Sep, close for 46 weeks by year's end.
  No once-frequent pair ever went five weeks apart: the cooling
  mechanism exists, but a district this small never leaves anyone
  alone long enough to trigger it. Same finding as the
  familiar-stranger CONCERN — the town is socially saturated.

In the routines:
  Sal kept only 59% of an autumn week into summer.
  Both newcomers were woven in within 3 weeks — Petra in September,
  Jack in February. The town absorbs anyone at the same speed; warm,
  but it is the same small-world saturation the familiar-stranger
  CONCERN has flagged since Phase 4.
  Median resident kept 73% of the autumn routine —
  lives persisted AND changed: neither frozen nor amnesiac.

In the places:
  Places acquired identities from use alone (see chronicle): the bakery a
  morning place, benches where peace gets made, the allotment breathing
  with the seasons (758 winter visits vs 2067 in spring).

In the calendar:
  21W 15D 16L over 52 matches — a fixture every Saturday, home and away alternating:
  the town's fortnight heartbeat never stopped, and the seasons turned
  around it. Christmas Day emptied the streets (85 outside vs 158
  the Sunday before);
  children chose the outdoors 11.1×/day in the holidays vs 1.9 in term.
  The year has a shape a returning player would recognise as A year,
  not A LOOP.

WHAT WOULD GIVE IT AWAY (honest limits)
  Nobody was born, nobody died, nobody moved away; one man retired but
  no one changed jobs, prices, or houses. The buildings did not weather.
  Children did not grow. A second identical seed would replay the same
  year exactly — which is a law (CD-007), not a flaw, but the DEMOGRAPHIC
  clock is still stopped even while the social clock now runs.

VERDICT
  YES for the social and seasonal year: relationships, grudges, routines,
  place identities and the calendar all accumulated twelve months of
  consequence, and the report above cites only computed facts.
  NOT YET for the demographic year: births, deaths, departures, growth.
  That is the next honest gap — exposed by simulation, not by concept.
