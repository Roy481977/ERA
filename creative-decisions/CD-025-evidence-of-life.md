# CD-025 — The town is continuously authored by the simulation

**Date:** 2026-07-26
**Status:** PROPOSED (from Roy's direction, verbatim below; awaiting ratification)
**Domain:** Design — world presentation / simulation

## The decision

The environment is not "decorated." Beyond the locked architecture (CD-024
track) and the chosen care layer (Sheet 03), the visible town is
**continuously written by the residents' lives** as *traces* — evidence of
life with an author, a cause and an exit. Roy: *"discovering how the lives of
the residents physically write themselves onto the town over time. That's
where the world will stop feeling handcrafted and start feeling alive."*

## Alternatives considered

- **More additive dressing** (Sheet 03 continued indefinitely). Rejected by
  Roy: everything additive is authored once; it cannot make the town feel
  alive over time.
- **Ambient random prop scatter** (industry default). Rejected outright by
  the iron rules: no author, no cause, no exit — clutter, not evidence.

## Why this wins

It is the mechanism by which ERA becomes unlike other games: the same street
is different on Tuesday morning, Tuesday evening, matchday and Sunday — and
different again next door — without one artist hour spent per instance. It
converts temperament into visible fact (the bin in on time; the trimmed
hedge), and it is cheap where ERA is strong: deterministic sim events driving
small enumerable prop states on plates.

## Consequences

- Buildings gain **named trace anchors** as a production requirement.
- The plate compositor gains a **dynamic prop layer** contract.
- Every new resident behaviour is asked: *what trace does it leave?*
- Acceptance test: a stranger can tell Tuesday from Friday, and this house
  from that one, from traces alone.
