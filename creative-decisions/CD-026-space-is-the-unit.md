# CD-026 — The primary design unit is space, not architecture

**Date:** 2026-07-27
**Status:** PROPOSED (from Roy's correction, verbatim below; awaiting ratification)
**Domain:** Design — world composition

## The decision

From this point onward, **place is the unit of design; buildings are
supporting actors.** Roy: *"An ERA town is remembered by the spaces between
buildings, not by the buildings themselves. … Stop starting every composition
with a house. Start with the place itself."* The Oak is a destination, not a
tree beside a house. The bakery is a square that smells of bread, not a shop.
The bridge is somewhere people stop, not infrastructure.

## What prompted it

Sheets 01–05 unconsciously anchored on the hero house — every Sheet 05
composition literally began with the house constructor and attached its place
to the side. The correction is structural, not cosmetic.

## Consequences

- Compositions begin from the void the walker moves through; buildings gain
  position/depth offsets so they can **enclose** a space rather than lead it.
- Sheet 05's verdicts stand, but its compositional method is superseded by
  this rule.
- First artefact under the rule: **Sheet 06 — The Walk Through Town**
  (`era-walk-v2.svg` / `.png`): one journey, nine beats, ground-level camera
  (view 34/6.5), the resident always walking left to right. Rhythm canon
  proposed: DOOR · QUIET STREET · BAKERY SQUARE · OAK · BRIDGE · HIGH STREET ·
  MARKET · LANE · GROUND — compression before every release, never two open
  beats in a row; the senses arrive before their sources (smell before the
  bakery, sound before the ground). Named weak transitions: 5→6 needs the
  Corner Wall as pivot; 3→4 needs the Notice Board as herald.
- Two cameras now exist in the language: **34/19** (production three-quarter,
  for sheets) and **34/6.5** (the resident's eyes, for experience studies).
