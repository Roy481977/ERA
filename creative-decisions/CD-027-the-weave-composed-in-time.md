# CD-027 — The Weave is ERA's canonical town composition, composed in space AND time

**Date:** 2026-07-27
**Status:** **RATIFIED (Roy, 2026-07-27)** — *"T9 – The Weave is the canonical
direction. … consider the composition question answered."*
**Domain:** Design — town structure / social simulation

## The decision

1. **Space:** every ERA town is composed as **The Weave** — a **daily line**
   (school · bakery · market) and a **weekly line** (homes · Oak · bridge ·
   ground) crossing at **one point**: the Oak, with the pub on the crossing's
   corner and quiet lanes ringing the edges. The ground sits across the water
   at the weekly line's end — floodlights and sound, never dominance; on
   matchday the bridge turns the town into one procession.
2. **Time (Roy's addition, canon):** *"A town is not only composed in space.
   It is also composed in time."* The same places belong to different people
   at different hours: the bakery to the morning, the school crossing to the
   afternoon, the pub to the evening, Stadium Road to matchday. **Movement
   answers where people meet; time answers when. Together they create the
   rhythm of the town.**

## Scope and closure

- Composition exploration is **closed**. The eight alternatives
  (`era-composition.svg`, T1–T8) are preserved **only as future town
  archetypes**, not as open questions. T3 and T6 remain rejected-for-record.
- District 01 is the Weave's crossing, zoomed in.

## Why it won (from the composition board)

Encounters are made by routines of *different frequency* sharing one node:
daily crosses weekly crosses seasonal at the Oak, so familiar strangers are
manufactured by geometry (CD-003 Recognition), friendships become accidents
of repetition (CD-001), quiet edges give dyads somewhere to become stories,
the school tide collides the generations twice a day, and deterministic
tides make watchable crowds (CD-006, watchability standard).

## First artefact under the ratified canon

**`era-district01-built.svg` / `.png`** — District 01 as ONE continuous
worldspace (production camera), Saturday 13:40: lanes, the crossing with its
Belisha beacons, Bakery Square, the Old Oak on its worn crossing of paths,
High Street (café · stores · post office), Market Square with the bus stop
on its edge and the stalls folding, The Home End facing both square and
Stadium Road, the bridge over the stream, and the ground held at the far
corner — sign, turnstiles and two floodlights only. The "Composed in Time"
strip beneath assigns each place its hour.

## Consequences

- All future districts and towns start from the Weave; variation happens in
  the places along the lines, never in the crossing structure.
- The time-of-day ownership table becomes a simulation contract: schedules
  should make each place *belong* to its hour (ties into CD-025's clocks).
- Next step per the board: residents — the town is ready to be lived in.
